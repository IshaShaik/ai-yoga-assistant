ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Yoga that feels personal.

AI Yoga Assistant combines browser-based camera tracking, voice coaching, pose guidance, music and progress tools in one focused wellness experience.

🎯 Live Posture Guidance

Each pose has a target guide and live body-position scoring to help you understand what to adjust while practicing.

🗣 Natural Voice Coaching

Choose English or Hinglish for spoken cues. Voice commands can also navigate supported practice actions hands-free.

🎵 Calming Music

Practice with the existing per-pose audio system, including its browser-generated ambient fallback.

📈 Progress You Can See

Completed sessions, practice time, streaks and AI-estimated accuracy are connected to the existing backend history.

AI supports your practice — it does not replace professional medical or fitness advice.
